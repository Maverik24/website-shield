## Types to look for

- Price and payment ("one flat price, paid upfront, quoted on the call")
- Guarantees and their conditions
- Definitions used by a guarantee
- Data handling (what a list is used for, who can access it, deletion timing)
- Messaging rules (consent, opt-out, sending window, carrier registration)
- Approval and control (client approves every message, can pause or delete)
- Timing ("emails usually go live within about a week of your approval")
- Product facts (page counts, channels included, add-ons)
- "We do not ..." statements (no trackers, no selling data)

## Rules

1. Only promise what is automatic or fully in the owner's control.
2. One exact wording per commitment; reuse it everywhere.
3. A short version of a guarantee always carries its limits and links to the full terms.
4. Before every deploy, search the built site for each commitment's key phrase and for any old wording that replaced it.
